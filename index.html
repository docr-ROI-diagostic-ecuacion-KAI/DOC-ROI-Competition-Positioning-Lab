03 · Competitive Marketing Matrix

Score observable activity across the journey.

Use 1–5 only when evidence exists. Use N/O when a signal cannot be observed. Unknown is not zero.

Scoring rule: 1 = Very low · 2 = Limited · 3 = Established · 4 = Strong · 5 = Category-leading · N/O = Not observable. Every score should be defensible with a source, date checked and a short justification.
04 · Read the matrix

METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

DIIIP explains how competitive evidence becomes a positioning decision

The lab is complete when observations are transformed into structured evidence, interpreted as competitive intelligence and converted into measurable positioning actions.

D
Data

Observable competitor signals: brands, URLs, sources, dates, journey activities, search presence, campaigns, audience environments and AI visibility.

I
Information

The evidence is structured into the competitive set, journey matrix and five positioning dimensions: Search, Demand, Paid, Audience and AI.

I
Intelligence

Comparable scores reveal competitor pressure, technique intensity, saturation and underused spaces without treating missing evidence as zero.

I
Insights

The diagnosis identifies relative strengths, visibility gaps, relevant whitespace and the evidence that should change the next marketing decision.

P
Personalization Actions

The findings become prioritized positioning actions with a KPI, baseline, target, owner and execution horizon for the defined Buyer Persona and market.
